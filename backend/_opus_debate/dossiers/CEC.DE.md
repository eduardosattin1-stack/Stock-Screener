# CECONOMY AG (CEC.DE, Xetra, EUR) - forensic dossier, 2026-11-09

Price used: EUR 4.53 on Xetra (FMP live quote, 9 November 2026; 52-week range EUR 3.51-4.59; market value about EUR 2.20 billion on about 485 million shares). Latest call transcript: second fiscal quarter, 13 May 2026.

## 1. NARRATIVE ARC
CECONOMY runs MediaMarkt and Saturn, Europe's largest consumer-electronics chain (about 1,000 stores, 11 countries, roughly EUR 24 billion of sales). The story management tells has moved from "turnaround retailer" to "platform": retail media (selling advertising to suppliers against store and app traffic), services, a third-party marketplace and rented floor space, now about 40% of gross profit. Since late 2025 the story has been overtaken by ownership: JD.com's 2025 tender at EUR 4.60 left JD.com and partner Convergenta with 85.2% on completion. The European Commission conditionally approved the purchase under the foreign-subsidies rules on 3 November 2026, and closing is expected by 12 November 2026, inside the 10 November-area long-stop the chief financial officer flagged on 13 May 2026.

Final figures are due 14 December 2026. Corroborated, though from trade press, not the company release.

The gap of roughly EUR 300 million a year between adjusted and reported profit recurs with a differently named one-off each year.

After rent, owner free cash flow is about EUR 200 million, in line with management's own guide: about 11 times at today's price.

## 6. RED FLAGS / GREEN FLAGS

## 7. HIDDEN SIGNALS

The market is pricing a delisting offer struck at or near EUR 4.60, not the business. Volume is low (about 5,000 shares on the quote day), so the marginal buyer is an event trader.

## 8. CAPITAL-ALLOCATION VERDICT (for the director)

Nothing here for a value book.
