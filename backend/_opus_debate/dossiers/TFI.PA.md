# TF1 Group (TFI.PA) - deep dive on management and the setup
*Transcripts reviewed: 9M-2025 (2025-10-30), FY-2025 (2026-02-12), H1-2026 (2026-07-30), 9M-2026 (2026-10-29). Price used: EUR 6.09 (Euronext Paris, FMP quote 2026-11-02).*

## 1. NARRATIVE ARC
A year ago (2025-10-30) the story was "resilience": revenue stable, operating margin held near 11%, market-share gains in a falling linear advertising market, and TF1+ (the free streaming platform) growing advertising revenue about 41%. By the full-year call (2026-02-12) the frame was "the second phase of the strategic plan" - micro-payments, the Netflix distribution deal, and French-speaking international expansion - while advertising revenue was already down 4% for 2025. By H1-2026 (2026-07-30) the story had turned defensive: the margin target was cut to "mid- to high single-digit", and net profit roughly halved. The 9M-2026 call (2026-10-29) is a "we are on the road map" call: margin from activities (current operating profit before one-offs, divided by revenue) of 7.6% versus 10.9% a year earlier, advertising revenue down 9%, and the growth vocabulary now leans almost entirely on the Netflix partnership, the small-business ad platform and micro-payments.

## 2. CLAIMS vs FINANCIALS (forensic)
| Claim | Evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| "Strong double-digit digital growth" | TF1+ ad revenue +19% over 9M-2026 (EUR 160M), +20.5% in Q3; total digital EUR 198M (+17%) | Delivered, but growth has halved from ~40% in 2025 |
| Digital offsets linear decline | Group advertising revenue EUR 1.0B, down 9% (about EUR 100M lost) versus roughly EUR 30M digital gain | Not true - the gap widened |
| Cost discipline protects margin | Programming costs down EUR 44M; margin still 7.6% vs 10.9% | Partial - costs cut, margin still fell about 3.3 points |
| "Very solid" balance sheet | Net cash EUR 382M at 2026-09-30 (EUR 587M cash vs EUR 207M debt at 2026-06-30) | True - the one clean pillar |
| "Growing dividend policy" | Dividend EUR 132M paid April 2026; free cash flow after working capital only EUR 16M over 9M-2026 (EUR 57M in H1, so Q3 was about minus EUR 41M) | Dividend is being paid out of the cash pile, not out of earnings |
| Earnings trajectory | EPS 0.97 (2024) to 0.72 (2025) to trailing 0.59; latest quarter EPS down 27% y/y | Decline is accelerating, not stabilising |

## 3. TONE & CONFIDENCE TRAJECTORY
Declining. 2025-10-30: "successfully managed to mitigate" ad headwinds. 2026-02-12: offensive, with return-on-investment slides for advertisers. 2026-07-30: defensive, margin guidance cut. 2026-10-29: "visibility remaining limited", the market forecast of minus 6% "now seems slightly optimistic", and for 2027 "we see no near-term prospects for improvement in the macroeconomic and political environment or any reduction in the regulatory asymmetries vis-a-vis global platforms." That last sentence is the most candid management has been in two years.

## 4. GUIDANCE CREDIBILITY
Medium. 2025 margin guidance was cut in October 2025 (from "broadly stable" to 10.5-11.5%) and then met at 11%. 2026 guidance was cut at H1 to mid- to high single-digit and is being met (7.6% at nine months). Management meets reset guidance; it does not forecast the down-leg well. The 9M-2026 results came in slightly above company-compiled consensus on revenue and margin.

## 5. ANALYST PRESSURE POINTS
Very thin coverage on the calls - the 9M-2026 call had only written questions from one broker (CIC). The questions were October/Q4 ad trends, 2027 visibility, the presidential election, Netflix monetisation, and programming costs. Management declined to give a programming-cost range and said Netflix revenue is ramping "more gradually than the audience" because ad load and CPM (price per thousand ad views) on Netflix are below TF1+ levels. Nobody asked about dividend cover, which is the real pressure point.

## 6. RED FLAGS / GREEN FLAGS
- Red: dividend (EUR 132M) is about eight times nine-month free cash flow after working capital (EUR 16M) - 9M-2026.
- Red: advertising revenue down 9% with management saying the market has "deteriorated further" since May - 9M-2026.
- Red: no improvement seen for 2027; the 2027 presidential election likely reduces prime-time ad inventory - 9M-2026.
- Red: Studio TF1 operating profit down EUR 14M over nine months - 9M-2026.
- Green: audience share at an all-time high of 36.9% among women under 50 in September 2026, and linear ad market share up about 2 points in August-September - 9M-2026.
- Green: Netflix partnership "ahead of our expectations" on audience; TF1+ growth re-accelerated to 20.5% in Q3 - 9M-2026.
- Green: net cash of EUR 382M is roughly 30% of the market value.

## 7. HIDDEN SIGNALS
The language moved from "gain market share" (a sign of strength in a stable market) to "regulatory asymmetries vis-a-vis global platforms" - an admission that the problem is structural, not cyclical. Disclosure has shifted toward audience and usage metrics (streamers, transactions) and away from digital revenue per user. The micro-payment figures (about 2 million transactions year to date) are small in euros; their prominence on the call is a sign of how few growth levers remain.

## 8. BOTTOM LINE
A well-run, market-leading broadcaster in a shrinking market. Management executes against reset targets, keeps a strong net cash position, and is honest about 2027. But the earnings base is still falling, digital growth is not filling the linear hole, and the dividend now relies on the balance sheet.
